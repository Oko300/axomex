The Lumina Waters

In the hidden depths of the Lumina Waters, 1,111 unique Axomex axolotls live peacefully among glowing reefs and ancient underwater cities. Each Axomex carries a rare elemental gift passed down from forgotten ocean guardians.

But when a mysterious darkness begins draining the life from their magical world, the 1,111 must unite to unlock the secrets buried beneath the abyss. From neon-powered warriors to crystal healers and shadow swimmers, every Axomex holds a piece of the prophecy that could either save their universe or destroy it forever.
